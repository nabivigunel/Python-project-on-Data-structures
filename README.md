# 🛒 Instacart Grocery Shopping Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Instacart Online Grocery Shopping Dataset using Python. The goal is to explore customer purchasing behavior, identify shopping trends, and gain insights into reorder patterns through data analysis and visualization.


## 📊 Dataset

The project uses the Instacart Online Grocery Shopping Dataset, which includes information about:

- Customers
- Orders
- Products
- Departments
- Aisles
- Reordered items


## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore customer shopping habits.
- Analyze shopping activity by hour of the day and day of the week.
- Identify the most popular products.
- Analyze reorder behavior.
- Calculate reorder proportions for products and customers.
- Visualize key findings using charts.


## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab


## 📈 Analysis Performed

During this project, I completed the following analyses:

- Data exploration and validation
- Missing value inspection
- Distribution of shopping hours
- Distribution of shopping days
- Days between consecutive orders
- Number of items per order
- Distribution of orders per customer
- Top 20 most ordered products
- Top 20 most reordered products
- Product reorder proportions
- Customer reorder proportions
- Shopping behavior comparison between weekdays



##  Key Findings

- Most grocery shopping occurs during daytime hours.
- Customer activity is relatively consistent throughout the week.
- Most customers purchase a small number of items per order.
- A limited number of products account for a large proportion of total orders.
- Frequently purchased products are also the most frequently reordered.
- Reorders represent a significant portion of customer purchases, indicating strong customer loyalty for commonly purchased products.
